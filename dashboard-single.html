How the number is made

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × patience factor.

  • Peer multiple: the median market-cap-to-TVL multiple of the tokens already trading in the protocol’s own category. A category with fewer than 3 live peers falls back to the whole-market median. Clamped to 0.02–2.00.
  • Growth factor: from the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+80%, worth 0.84× to 1.32×. No reported change counts as flat.
  • Patience factor: from how long the protocol has been tracked with no token recorded — 0.75 for a new listing, rising to 1.00 after three years. Unknown listing date counts 0.85.
  • Confidence follows the peer sample: high with 8+ peers and a known listing date, medium with 3+, otherwise low.

How rough the yardstick is

Testing the yardstick…

Every implied value is shown as a range, not just a point: the same arithmetic with the peer multiple at its 25th and 75th percentile, so the spread of the peers is visible instead of hidden. The test behind the range is a leave-one-out check on the tokens already trading: each one is priced from its peers excluding itself and compared with its real market cap. The yardstick is unbiased on the median but rough — a typical error of several-fold — which is why the board ranks by the point estimate and shows the range beside it. The growth and patience tilts are small on purpose: on the same test they do not beat the peer multiple alone, so they tilt the price instead of driving it.

Launch odds and the money view

Launch odds are the peers’ own launch record: the share of the snapshot’s protocols in the protocol’s category that already record a token, shrunk toward the whole-market rate so a small category never reads 0% or 100%. The odds do not change the rank — rank is the implied value — but they change the decision: the board’s top rows sit in Risk Curators, the category least likely to ever launch a token, and the odds say so plainly. Each protocol’s detail view carries the money view: the odds-weighted farmer pool at a stated 5% farmer share of the implied value, and what farming costs in gas and hours. The pool is the whole prize at those odds, before it is split across every farmer — it is never divided by hours into a wage, because no single farmer takes it all.

The snapshot says a protocol records no token — never that it has none. A protocol on the list may still hold a token its DefiLlama record does not carry. Implied value is this board’s own claim, not a forecast and not financial advice: it does not predict whether or when any token is issued. Farm steps are a starting plan, not a guarantee of any reward.
